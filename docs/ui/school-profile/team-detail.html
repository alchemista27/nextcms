About Me

Passionate About Making Science Accessible

With over 12 years of experience in science education, I have dedicated my career to making complex scientific concepts engaging and accessible to students of all learning styles. My philosophy is grounded in inquiry-based learning — letting students discover, hypothesize, and experiment.

I hold a PhD in Biochemistry from MIT and have published several research papers on sustainable chemistry methods. Before joining SMaRT School, I worked as a research scientist at a leading biotech firm, an experience that gives my teaching a real-world perspective that students truly appreciate.
